SkillzStorm Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

SkillzStorm is an educational gaming platform. We are committed to child privacy and COPPA compliance.

Data Practices

  • No account creation required for gameplay.
  • No collection of child personal identifiers by SkillzStorm.
  • Gameplay progress/settings are stored locally on-device.

Advertising

  • Google AdMob child-directed treatment enabled.
  • Max ad content rating configured to General (G).
  • No behavioral targeting by SkillzStorm for child users.

Public policy references:

  • AdMob child-directed treatment (COPPA)
  • AdMob Families policy compliance
  • Google ad moderation process (automation + human review)
  • Google ad review process
